# 04 — Edit future weekly plans

**What to build:** A user can edit training blocks and the future weekly plan with immediate save, duplication, reordering, and one-step undo, while historical plan occurrences remain unchanged.

**Blocked by:** 02 — First-run initialization and weekly plan display; 03 — Exercise library management.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A user can add, edit, remove, reorder, and configure exercises in a training block.
- [ ] A user can assign training blocks, cardio, or rest to future plan days.
- [ ] Blocks and weekly plans can be duplicated.
- [ ] Saves are immediate and expose success or failure state.
- [ ] Undo restores the most recent plan edit.
- [ ] Past plan occurrences and training history do not change after future edits.
