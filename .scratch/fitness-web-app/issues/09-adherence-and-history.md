# 09 — Adherence and history

**What to build:** A user can understand both whether the plan was followed and how measured performance changed over time, without mixing those concepts.

**Blocked by:** 05 — Quick workout completion; 06 — Detailed set-level training records; 07 — Partial completion, rescheduling, and substitutions.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Recent training records can be viewed and filtered by exercise and date range.
- [ ] Statistics distinguish planned, completed, partial, skipped, rescheduled, and rest outcomes.
- [ ] Training frequency and completed training days are visible.
- [ ] Measured performance trends group by actual exercise.
- [ ] Quick completions without measurements do not create false performance points.
- [ ] Substitutions count toward adherence without merging unlike exercise trends.
